Semester-wise Curriculum

Semester 1: Management Foundations

Principles of Management, Financial Accounting, Organizational Behavior, Managerial Economics, Business Communication, and IT for Managers.

Semester 2: Core Functional Areas

Marketing Management, Human Resource Management, Operations Management, Financial Management, Business Research Methods, and Legal Aspects of Business.

Semester 3: Specialization & Electives

Specialization-specific courses, Strategic Management, Business Ethics, Elective papers, Summer Internship Project (SIP), and Industry Interface.

Semester 4: Capstone & Industry

Management Project, Comprehensive Viva, Industry Mentorship, Elective specialization, Business Simulation, and Placement Preparation.

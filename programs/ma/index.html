Program Overview

The M.A. programs at DSVV blend rigorous academic study with the university's emphasis on cultural values, philosophical inquiry, and holistic development. Students engage with both classical Indian knowledge systems and contemporary academic frameworks.

Semester-wise Curriculum

Semester 1: Core Foundations

Advanced theory papers in chosen specialization, research methodology, Indian philosophical foundations, and seminar presentations.

Semester 2: Specialized Studies

In-depth specialization papers, elective courses, comparative studies, and practical application workshops.

Semester 3: Advanced Research

Research methods, advanced elective papers, field study component, and preliminary dissertation planning.

Semester 4: Dissertation

Dissertation research, comprehensive examination, viva voce, and presentation of research findings.

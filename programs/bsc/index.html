Program Overview

The Bachelor of Science (B.Sc.) programs at Dev Sanskriti Vishwavidyalaya are designed to integrate modern scientific education with the university's core philosophy of holistic development. Students receive rigorous academic training combined with value-based learning, practical laboratory experience, and character-building modules.

DSVV's B.Sc. programs emphasize experiential learning through mandatory practical sessions, field visits, community outreach, and research projects alongside conventional coursework.

Semester-wise Curriculum

Semester 1 & 2: Foundation Sciences

Introduction to core scientific principles, general chemistry, physics fundamentals, biology basics, English communication, and environmental studies. Foundation modules in computer literacy.

Semester 3 & 4: Core Discipline

In-depth study of chosen specialization with advanced theoretical coursework, laboratory practicals, statistical methods, and research methodology introduction.

Semester 5 & 6: Advanced Studies & Project

Specialized advanced topics, elective papers, dissertation/project work, internship component, and comprehensive viva voce examination.

Semester-wise Curriculum

Semester 1: Core Foundations

Advanced core theory papers, research methodology introduction, practical laboratory sessions, and seminars on current developments in the field.

Semester 2: Specialized Studies

Advanced specialization papers, elective courses, computer applications in science, and analytical techniques laboratory work.

Semester 3: Research Methods

Advanced elective papers, research methods and statistics, instrumentation practicals, and preliminary dissertation work.

Semester 4: Dissertation

Full-time dissertation research under faculty guidance, comprehensive viva voce, and research publication support.

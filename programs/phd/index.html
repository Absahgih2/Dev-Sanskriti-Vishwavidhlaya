Program Overview

The Ph.D. programs at DSVV provide a rigorous research environment across 18+ disciplines. Scholars work under experienced faculty supervisors, utilizing the university's research centers and laboratory infrastructure to produce original contributions to knowledge.

DSVV's doctoral program emphasizes both academic excellence and practical relevance, encouraging interdisciplinary research that addresses real-world challenges.

Program Structure

Year 1: Coursework & Research Foundation

Research Methodology coursework, Literature Review, Research Proposal development, Subject-specific advanced courses, and Preliminary examination preparation.

Year 2: Active Research

Original research work, Data collection & analysis, Conference presentations, Publication in peer-reviewed journals, and Progress review seminars.

Year 3: Thesis & Defense

Thesis writing, Final review, Thesis submission, Viva Voce examination, and Public defense before expert panel.
